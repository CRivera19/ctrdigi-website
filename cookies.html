Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

The short version

This website does not set any cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and device fingerprinting. They are often used to remember preferences, measure visits or show ads.

What this website uses

  • Cookies: none.
  • Local storage or similar: none.
  • Analytics or advertising tools: none.
  • Third-party content: fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load but does not set cookies for this. See our Privacy Policy.

If this changes

If we add any non-essential cookies or tracking in the future, we will update this page first and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

Controlling cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Most browsers also support Global Privacy Control, which we will honor if we ever add tracking.
